`Delete` or `Backspace` (the key a Mac labels "delete") resets the focused Knob, Slider, Fader, ValueField, XYPad, draggable Plot marker or EnvelopeEditor handle to its default, in one gesture: the keyboard's reset, beside the screen reader's reset action. With Command, Control or Alt held, the keys are left to shortcuts of their own; the headless hooks get it too.
