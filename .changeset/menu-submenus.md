Add submenus to DropdownMenu and ContextMenu: `Menu.Sub`, `Menu.SubTrigger` and `Menu.SubContent`, nesting to any depth. A submenu opens on a short rest or a press, or from the keyboard, and stays open while the pointer heads for it across the rows in between. The `Menu` theme gains `chevron` and `itemOpen` slots.
