`Tooltip` enhances one control that forwards a Soundor node ref. It appears on
hover or keyboard-visible focus. `openDelay` and `closeDelay` are milliseconds
and default to 500 and 100. `side`, `align` and `offset` choose the preferred
position; the surface flips and clamps at the editor's edge. `content` may
change while open, for example to show a Knob's current value. Pressing or
starting a drag hides an automatic tooltip until a new hover or focus.

Supply `open` to own visibility. For a Plot marker or other external anchor,
use `<Tooltip open={showDescription} anchor={markerRef} content={description} />`.
External points, rectangles and dynamic readers use logical view coordinates.
Hide controlled hover tips on gesture start or use controlled visibility to
show a live readout during a gesture.

Content is plain text and does not receive pointer input or focus. Soundor
currently has no tooltip role or description relationship, so for compatible
controls the text is also supplied as the accessibility hint (an explicitly
provided hint takes precedence). Always give icon-only controls a meaningful
label. Disabled controls may receive hover but cannot take keyboard focus;
make essential explanations available independently of the tooltip.

Styling slots are `root` and `label` under `theme.components.Tooltip`.
