Use `Toggle` for on/off state. Bind it to a boolean parameter or control it with `value` and `onChange`.

<Demo name="toggle" height={150} />

## Keyboard and accessibility

A Toggle is a tab stop, and `Enter` or `Space` switches it. It is announced as a switch or a toggle button, depending on `variant`, with its on or off state. See [Keyboard and accessibility](/docs/guides/keyboard-and-accessibility).
