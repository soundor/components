---
title: SelectableList
description: A row or column of selectable items.
---

Use `SelectableList` for visible choices such as bands, presets, channels or modulation sources.

<Demo name="selectable-list" height={180} />

```tsx
<SelectableList
  accessibilityLabel="Bands"
  orientation="horizontal"
  items={bands.map((band) => ({ id: band.id, label: band.name }))}
  selectedIds={selectedIds}
  onSelectionChange={setSelectedIds}
/>
```

The caller owns `selectedIds`. Use stable IDs, give each item a label, and set
`disabled` for unavailable entries. `multiple` makes activation toggle items
independently. The default orientation is vertical. Each enabled item is
focusable; arrows along the layout, Home and End move focus without changing
selection. Enter, Space, a press or assistive activation selects the option.

`renderItem(item, state)` replaces the visual content of an item. Keep it
non-interactive; the list supplies the option's role, label and selected state.
Style the `root`, `item` and `label` slots with `theme.components.SelectableList`,
`style`, or `styles`.

Import `useSelection` from `@soundor/components/headless` for a custom layout.
It accepts the same selection, items and orientation options. Forward
`attach(id, node)` to each focusable item, `onKeyDown(id, event)` to its key
handler, and `select(item)` to its activation handler. Supply the roles,
labels and focus styles appropriate for the custom presentation.
